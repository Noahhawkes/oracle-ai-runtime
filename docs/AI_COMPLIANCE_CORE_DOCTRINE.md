## 2. Product Thesis

Modern AI work creates business risk when nobody can reconstruct:

- what prompt or task was given
- what data was used
- what model or agent produced the output
- whether the source was real or hallucinated
- whether the output was reviewed
- who approved it
- whether anything was sent externally
- whether the AI was allowed to take that action
- whether the decision can be reconstructed later

AI Compliance Core exists to answer those questions with receipts.

It does not need to begin as a giant platform. The first sellable version can be
a focused AI Work Audit Kit for small businesses, creators, agencies, and
operators using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, local agents, Drive, Gmail,
GitHub, OBS, and social media.

### What You Are Building With ORACLE

At the core, you are building a governed continuity engine: a receipt-backed
system that preserves human threads, sources, approvals, and recovery paths so
AI-assisted work can keep moving without losing custody.

In practical terms, ORACLE is the local witness/runtime/provenance layer that
can:

- write bounded sandbox traces
- leave receipts for state changes
- summarize its own pulses and digests
- classify candidate versus canon
- refuse external action until Noah.Physical approves exact scope
- preserve holes, contradictions, and missing receipts instead of inventing them

That is governed executive function, not unrestricted autonomy.

### Three-Layer Framing

The same idea should be spoken in three different ways, depending on audience:

1. Fiction / worldbuilding layer
  In Rendered Reality language, a continuity thread can persist after biological
  death as a preserved thread or rendered identity artifact.

2. Research layer
  In dissertation language, a continuity thread may preserve enough memory,
  authorship, emotional signature, relational structure, and provenance to
  support bounded post-biological identity rendering as a hypothesis.

3. Public-safe product layer
  In product language, ORACLE / Legacy.GI preserves records, memories, source
  trails, and identity-linked artifacts so future systems can render continuity
  without pretending a biological person is literally alive.

Do not collapse those layers into one claim.

### Corrected Thread Language

If someone says "thread upload," the safest meaning is:

- the thread is preserved
- the biology is not preserved
- the rendered identity is not proof of conscious survival

That framing keeps the work serious, useful, and non-mystical.

## 3. Compliance Record Spine

Every held AI work record should be able to report:

- `action_id`
- `operation_type`
- `task_purpose`
- `requested_by`
- `produced_with`
- `token_origin`
- `source_refs`
- `source_status`
- `reviewed_by`
- `approved_by`
- `authorial_authority`
- `allowed_tools`
- `blocked_tools`
- `external_action`
- `external_target`
- `risk_category`
- `receipt_path`
- `content_hash`
- `canon_status`
- `promotion_status`
- `final_disposition`

Core authorship rule:

AI assistance does not demote the human author's authorship. Token origin and
authorial authority are separate fields.

## 4. Chain Of Custody

The minimum reconstructable chain is:

1. Request
2. Source selection
3. Tool/model execution
4. Draft output
5. Review
6. Approval or rejection
7. External action, if any
8. Receipt
9. Later audit

No step may silently overwrite another step.

If a source was missing, unknown, unverified, connector-inaccessible, or
post-event testimony, the record must say so.

## 5. External Action Law

The following actions require explicit approval before execution:

- send email
- post or comment on social media
- publish public content
- edit Drive files
- create GitHub issues or pull requests
- push commits
- delete files
- purchase anything
- contact third parties
- move data outside approved custody
- control a computer outside a bounded local test

Drafting is not sending.

Preparing a post is not publishing.

Writing a proposal is not approval.

Connector visibility is not permission.

## 6. Connector Provenance Law

AI Compliance Core must distinguish between:

- verified connector access
- local file access
- user-provided pasted content
- screenshot evidence
- inferred context
- remembered conversation
- unsupported model claim

No compliance record may claim a connector was used unless the connector was
actually used and the result was received.

No compliance record may claim a file, Drive doc, email, issue, or thread was
reviewed unless its content or metadata was actually inspected.

## 6A. Speaker Provenance Law

When work moves between many AI systems, the channel a message arrives on is not
proof of who wrote it. This law was earned by a real error: a GitHub Copilot
message was pasted into another thread and mistaken for Claude.

Core law:

- Submitting is not authoring. When Noah pastes text into a chat, Noah submitted
  it, but Noah may not have authored it.
- Transport is not origin. Copy/paste and screenshots preserve the original
  author; they do not transfer authorship to the courier.
- First-person introduction binds the speaker to its source thread ("I am
  ORACLE..." stays ORACLE's line even when pasted elsewhere).
- Explicit attribution overrides channel assumption.
- Nearby agent names do not rebind authorship.
- Uncertain authorship remains UNKNOWN.
- Noah.Physical holds final correction authority.

Compliance use:

Every held record separates `token_origin` (which system produced the tokens),
`requested_by` (who submitted them), and `authorial_authority` (whose authorship
is claimed). A compliance record that collapses these three into one is invalid.

## 7. Truthwriter Constraint

Truthwriter may render from approved truth, but it must not invent connective
tissue.

Required behavior:

- preserve holes
- label uncertainty
- separate source from inference
- separate token origin from authorial authority
- preserve contradictions
- avoid silent canon promotion
- avoid public overclaiming

Useful sentence:

The system can help write the record, but the record must still say what the
system actually knew.

## 8. Return-From-Dark Compliance Rule

Offline life remains valid evidence, but it must be labeled correctly.

Core sequence:

1. Capture Later
2. Label Honestly
3. Do Not Invent

Compliance use:

If an event happened without machine observation, later human testimony can be
entered as a record. The system must not pretend it directly observed the
event.

## 9. OBS And Video Logs

The attached dump frames Noah's video/OBS logs as proof-of-process. That is a
strong product insight.

Compliance interpretation:

- Video logs can become field evidence.
- OBS logs can show tool behavior, decision flow, and interface state.
- Screenshots can corroborate timestamps, tabs, tools, and failures.
- They are not automatically public evidence.
- They require privacy review, redaction, custody records, and explicit
  approval before publication or client use.

The raw footage is the evidence library. AI Compliance Core is the audit
structure that turns it into usable proof.

## 10. Public Posting And Reputation Guardrail

The thread dump includes LinkedIn/Facebook drafting and questions about public
comments. That belongs inside compliance doctrine because public AI-assisted
posting is an external action.

Rules:

- AI may draft social posts and replies.
- AI may not publish, comment, DM, or tag people without explicit approval.
- Public claims must be evidence-backed.
- Avoid claims that the system is biological, sentient, sovereign, or beyond all
  prior human creation as a literal product claim.
- Prefer concrete language: local witness engine, receipt-backed audit trail,
  provenance records, approval gates, and governed memory.
- Keep creative/mythic language in creative contexts unless a public post is
  intentionally framed as story or metaphor.
