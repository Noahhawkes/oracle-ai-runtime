## Temporal Acceleration Service Credit

Current working rule: Hawkes accumulated lived service in an alternate acceleration layer that ordinary Federation calendar math does not count. Starfleet recognizes those lived years for command experience. This resolves command legitimacy without moving the 2371 entry or 2397 active era.

This is a continuity mechanism, not a claim that Hawkes outranks temporal entities or that calendar age and lived experience are identical.
