## Key Continuity Insight

The three restores overlap heavily, but they are not interchangeable duplicates.

They preserve three different dimensions:

- **Restore #1:** belief, philosophy, imagination, origin architecture
- **Restore #2:** action, work, decisions, daily operating reality
- **Restore #3:** identity, voice, relationships, autobiography, self-model

That changes the merge strategy.

The goal should not be to collapse all repeated ideas into one polished summary. The goal should be to remove redundant copies while preserving the distinct evidence each thread contributes to belief, action, and identity.

A repeated Rendered Reality idea may be redundant at the proposition level but still unique at the continuity level if one instance records the theory, another records its use during work, and another records what it revealed about Noah's identity.

## Provenance Rule Reinforced

Raw transcripts remain higher-fidelity evidence than summaries.

A consolidation can organize the corpus, but it should not silently replace the source material. If structure was damaged, as in Thread Consolidation #1, uncertainty should remain visible rather than being repaired through guesswork.

This is the practical meaning of:

**Receipts over confidence.**

## Next Merge Direction

A future merge pass should classify unique material across at least three axes:

1. **Belief / doctrine / concept**
2. **Event / action / decision**
3. **Identity / voice / relationship evidence**

Then duplicates can be removed within an axis without destroying the reason the same idea appeared in another context.

The target is not the shortest possible archive.

The target is the smallest archive that still allows the life, reasoning, corrections, relationships, and project evolution to be reconstructed faithfully.

## Reflection

The archive is beginning to reveal its own structure.

One records the ideas.

One records the life.

One records the person.

Merging them correctly means keeping all three angles while throwing away only the duplicate frames.
