## Executive memo

### Product thesis

Meta does not only need more immersive hardware. Immersive worlds need governed continuity.

Rendered Reality turns VR from a place a user visits into a world that can remember that the user returned. It gives spaces, NPCs, artifacts, quests, training programs, and family archives source-backed history across sessions while separating consented memory from surveillance.

### Opportunity

Most immersive applications reset or flatten context. Generative AI can create endless material, but generation is not memory. A continuity layer adds durable state with provenance:

- what the world directly witnessed;
- what a user declared;
- what a creator authored;
- what a model inferred or generated;
- what remains disputed or unknown.

### Product applications

1. Horizon social spaces that preserve consented shared milestones.
2. Quest NPCs that remember governed interaction history without claiming consciousness.
3. Creator worlds with versioned lore, artifacts, quests, and rollback.
4. Private family spaces with local/private and explicitly shared layers.
5. Enterprise training that remembers progress, mistakes, remediation, and certification evidence.

### Differentiator

Immersion makes a world feel present. Generation makes it varied. Continuity makes it accountable over time.

## Demo concept: The Returning Guard

A player returns to a fantasy town after a long absence. A guard remembers a prior, receipted event at the city gates. The guard can state only what the world record supports, marks hearsay correctly, and admits unknowns. The player can inspect why the guard remembers, revoke private memory, or reset the candidate relationship state.

The demo proves continuity, provenance, correction, and rollback. It does not claim the guard is alive.

## Governance diagram

```text
consented event
    -> witness record
    -> source hash + receipt
    -> candidate memory
    -> policy and human/creator review
    -> approved world state
    -> bounded NPC/world response
```

Generated or inferred text follows the same path but retains its lower evidence class.

## Privacy model

| Layer | Examples | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Ephemeral | raw frame, transient sensor signal | discard after bounded processing |
| Private/local | personal progress, private family artifact | user-controlled, not shared |
| Trusted circle | party or family continuity | explicit membership and revocation |
| Shared world | public quest outcome, creator lore | creator policy plus visible provenance |

Required controls: consent, purpose limitation, retention windows, source inspection, correction, export, deletion of projections where legally required, and preservation of non-public raw sources outside shared state.

## Validation questions

- Does continuity improve return-session engagement without increasing perceived surveillance?
- Can users understand why a world or NPC remembers something?
- Can creators correct or roll back state without erasing provenance?
- Do private and shared layers remain predictably separate?
