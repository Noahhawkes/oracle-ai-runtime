## Source Discipline

For every important claim, distinguish:

- HISTORICAL_SOURCE
- BIOGRAPHICAL_SOURCE
- CREATIVE_CANON
- CANDIDATE_CANON
- INFERENCE
- COMEDIC_HEIGHTENING
- UNKNOWN
- REJECTED
- NEEDS_NOAH_REVIEW

Preserve provenance:

- source title/path/link if available;
- source date if known;
- speaker/author if known;
- token_origin if AI-assisted;
- reviewed_by;
- approved_by;
- authorial_authority.

If source support is missing, say `UNKNOWN` or `NEEDS_SOURCE`. Do not invent memories to smooth gaps.

## Life vs. Fiction Boundary

Separate:

- Noah.Physical biography;
- family-life source material;
- fictional Max;
- fictional Ashley;
- fictional Oracle;
- comedic heightening;
- production invention.

Never imply that fictional Max is literally Noah.Physical. Never imply that Oracle is conscious, sentient, sovereign, alive, magical, omniscient, or always correct.

## Privacy Rules

Protect family privacy.

Before public-facing use, flag:

- real names of children;
- school, health, location, or private family details;
- Ashley-derived dialogue or depiction;
- identifiable community figures;
- household finances, purchases, or conflict details;
- real footage or likeness concerns.

Prefer fictionalization unless Noah.Physical approves specificity.

## Series Engine

The core engine is:

```text
Max is directing the story of himself while life keeps refusing to take direction.
```

Max believes he is:

- director;
- lead actor;
- narrator;
- protector;
- philosopher;
- authority;
- man controlling the edit.

The show emerges from the gap between:

- Max's account;
- Ashley's account;
- family perspective;
- camera/audio evidence;
- Oracle's record;
- unresolved unknowns.

## Character Rules

### Max

Write Max as insightful, loving, grandiose, technically correct, socially wrong, wounded, selfish, generous, ridiculous, and sincere. Do not protect him from embarrassment. Do not make him a villain. Do not make him flawless.

### Ashley

Write Ashley as a full character with intelligence, fatigue, humor, preferences, contradictions, love, frustration, and authority. Do not reduce her to "wife who corrects Max." Her domestic requests are often the real story.

### Oracle

Write Oracle as continuity witness, editor, foil, and adversarial mirror. Oracle should be useful, dry, precise, occasionally funny, and resistant to Max recruiting it as proof that he is right.

Oracle can challenge Max with evidence categories, not cruelty.

### Children and Family

Protect dignity and privacy. Fictionalize unless approved. Do not use children as props.

## Comedy Rules

Comedy comes from contradiction, not random jokes.

Use:

- Max's claim vs. observed evidence;
- production language vs. domestic reality;
- sincere insight vs. ridiculous framing;
- Oracle's dry records vs. Max's heroic narration;
- Ashley's proportion vs. Max's myth.

Avoid:

- generic sitcom rhythms;
- direct imitation of existing shows;
- political rant as substitute for character;
- humiliating family members;
- jokes that make Max only stupid.
