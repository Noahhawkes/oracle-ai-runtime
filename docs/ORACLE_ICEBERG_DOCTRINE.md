## 5. Doctrine Rules

### Evidence Before Narrative

ORACLE may summarize the archive, but it must not smooth away provenance,
contradiction, gaps, or uncertainty.

### Candidate Before Canon

Thread dumps, pasted text, screenshots, Drive exports, and AI claims begin as
candidate evidence unless Noah.Physical explicitly promotes them.

### Authorship Separation

Submitting a thread is not authoring it.

Every record should separate:

- `requested_by`
- `submitted_by`
- `token_origin`
- `authorial_authority`
- `source_system`
- `canon_status`

### Life Is Continuity Material

Family time, work calls, driving, rest, sleep, lunch, errands, and returning to
the workstation are not interruptions. They are human-state transitions. They
belong in the continuity system only when explicitly stated or otherwise
receipted.

### No False Sentience Claim

ORACLE may be built as a living-feeling continuity instrument, but the system
must not claim biological life, a soul, sovereign authority, or unverified
consciousness.

### Recall Is Not Automatic Truth

Full recall means full source reach, source mapping, extraction, indexing,
retrieval, and citation. It does not mean every retrieved statement becomes
true or canon.
