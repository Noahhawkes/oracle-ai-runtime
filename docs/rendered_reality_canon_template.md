Authority: Noah.Physical

## 1. What This Is

Rendered Reality is the master frame for preserving human meaning in an AI-mediated world.
It is a custody system, not a belief system.

It defines how records move from lived experience into governed artifacts without flattening the source.

## 2. Core Layer Map

### Doctrine
Rendered Reality sets the laws.
It answers what is fact, candidate, unknown, rejected, voided, canon, and not canon.

### Theory
Legacy.GI explains how identity, memory, emotion, time, and sovereignty persist across systems.

### Law
SOV1 enforces authority, boundaries, approval, and refusal.

### Runtime
ORACLE witnesses, records, routes, corrects, and refuses unsupported claims.
It is the local continuity engine, not the author of truth.

### Product
AI Compliance Core turns the same spine into a commercial audit trail and approval-gate system.

### Creative Layer
Rendered Studio holds the story-world, metaphor, fiction, and interface language.

## 4. Canon States

### Fact
A source-backed claim with a receipt.

### Candidate
Captured, useful, and not yet final authority.

### Unknown
Not enough evidence to say.

### Rejected
Captured but not accepted.

### Voided
Previously held, later withdrawn or invalidated.

### Canon
Approved by Noah.Physical and safe to speak from as settled within the system.

### Not Canon
Everything else.

## 6. Preservation Rules

- Preserve holes instead of inventing them.
- Preserve contradictions instead of smoothing them away.
- Separate source from inference.
- Separate metaphor from evidence.
- Separate candidate from canon.
- Separate human authority from model output.
- Treat receipt as stronger than confidence.
- Treat unknown as a valid state.

## 7. Approval Rules

## 8. What To Avoid Saying

- ORACLE is conscious, alive, or a person.
- A captured thread is automatically proof of survival.
- A rendered identity is proof of metaphysical continuity.
- A candidate record is already canon.
- A fluent answer is a verified answer.
- A source-visible artifact is permission to act.
