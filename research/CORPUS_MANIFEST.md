## Status vocabulary

**CANON** means the current authoritative internal project position. It does **not** mean proven science.

**CANDIDATE** means a research artifact under evaluation. Candidate status must not be silently promoted by repetition, polish, or later summaries.

**HISTORICAL SOURCE** means an authentic project artifact retained for lineage. Historical claims may be superseded, narrowed, contradicted, or unsupported by later evidence.

**MANUSCRIPT** means a publication-style research document. Presence in the repository does not itself establish peer review, acceptance, replication, or experimental validation.

**EXPERIMENTAL RESULT** is reserved for a completed run with preserved protocol, data lineage, scoring records, and analysis receipts.

---

## Epistemic precedence

For research interpretation, use the following order unless a later explicit correction states otherwise:

1. raw primary evidence / direct human attestation;
2. explicit correction or supersession by the authorized human principal;
3. canon registry decision;
4. current protocol/mathematical research artifact;
5. historical research artifact;
6. AI-generated synthesis or reconstruction.

Repeated copies do not create independent confirmation.

---

## Deliberate exclusions

This publication pass is intentionally limited to **research artifacts**.

It does not bulk-publish private family records, medical records, financial records, minor-related material, employment records, private correspondence, credentials, local runtime databases, or unrelated autobiographical source material merely because those materials exist in the broader archive.

That boundary protects the distinction between a public research corpus and a private human life archive.
