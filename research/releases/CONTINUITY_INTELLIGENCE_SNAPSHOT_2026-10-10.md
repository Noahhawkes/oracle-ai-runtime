## Narrow current claim

> **Selective structure-preserving compression may retain longitudinal human continuity information better than strong generic compression at equal information budgets.**

This is a falsifiable research hypothesis.

It is not a claim that the hypothesis has passed an experiment.

## Six operational dimensions

The current first-phase measurement ruler contains six dimensions:

1. **Decision fidelity** — preserve what was chosen, by whom, under what constraints, and whether the decision remained in force.
2. **Correction fidelity** — preserve prior state, correction event, authority, supersession, and current state without rewriting history as always-correct.
3. **Temporal fidelity** — preserve ordering, state-at-time, historical/current distinction, and relevant duration.
4. **Relational fidelity** — preserve relationship-dependent structure and role boundaries without substituting one person or relationship for another.
5. **Provenance fidelity** — preserve source, authorship/attribution where material, transformation chain, and evidence lineage.
6. **Uncertainty fidelity** — preserve unknowns, conflicts, confidence limits, and unresolved states instead of smoothing them into certainty.

These six dimensions are a **measurement ruler for the present hypothesis**. They are not claimed to be a complete theory of human identity.

## Failure standard

The method must be allowed to lose.

A clean negative result remains a negative result if the experiment was validly executed and the continuity-aware condition fails the frozen success criteria.

The project may not rename a valid loss merely because the result is undesirable.

A failed implementation does not disprove every possible continuity-aware method, but it does falsify the claim that the tested implementation succeeded under the locked conditions.
