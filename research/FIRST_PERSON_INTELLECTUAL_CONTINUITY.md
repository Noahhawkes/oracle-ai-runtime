## 1. The principle

Noah Hawkes developed his ideas through sustained human-AI collaboration. AI-assisted drafting, formatting, expansion, formalization, mathematical development, and narrative expansion do not automatically diminish his authorship.

**Authorship is not the same as keystroke provenance.**

An AI-assisted artifact can represent Noah Hawkes's authored thought when its conversational history, authorial direction, adoption, revision, reaffirmation, or subsequent use establishes that relationship. The use of AI to express or develop a thought does not, by itself, invalidate its human authorship.

Rejected rule: "AI typed the sentence, therefore it is not Noah's thought."

---

## 2. The bidirectional guard

The principle works in both directions, and both directions must be enforced:

**a. Against demotion.** Adopted work must not be arbitrarily demoted merely because AI supplied the surface wording. Where sustained direction, acceptance, revision, reuse, and incorporation are evidenced, the work stands as Noah's authored thought regardless of which agent produced the keystrokes.

**b. Against automatic attribution.** A transferred passage must not automatically be treated as Noah's personal belief simply because he pasted it. Noah frequently acted as a transmitter of other systems' material for analysis. The complete conversational history — the prompt, the transport role, any framing, and what followed — determines the relationship, not the presence of the text in his channel.

---

## 3. Four separations

This doctrine separates four questions that must never be collapsed into one:

1. **Authorship:** Whose intellectual work is this? Did Noah originate, direct, adopt, correct, or continue it?
2. **Wording provenance:** How was the surface text produced? (direct typing, dictation, AI drafting, AI-assisted polishing, AI formalization, transcription, transmission of another's text)
3. **Empirical validity:** Is the claim demonstrated, testable, refuted, or speculative? Authorship does not confer empirical validity. A claim can be genuinely Noah's without being scientifically proven.
4. **Current status:** Is this a current adopted position, a superseded historical state, a candidate, or unknown? Later refinement does not erase earlier thought; earlier thought does not bind current position.

**Direction/adoption labels (two-axis, production classes separate):**

- NOAH-ORIGINATED CONCEPT — the concept traces to Noah's initiation.
- NOAH-DIRECTED DEVELOPMENT — Noah instructed the development path.
- AI-ASSISTED FORMULATION — AI produced surface wording under Noah's direction.
- NOAH-ADOPTED SYNTHESIS — Noah accepted, revised, or reaffirmed an AI-shaped formulation; both layers remain visible.
- JOINT HUMAN-AI EXPLORATION — substantive development by both, origin not cleanly separable.
- LATER NOAH CORRECTION — supersedes an earlier state; the earlier state is preserved, not erased.
- SUPERSEDED HISTORICAL STATE — genuine earlier thought, no longer current.
- TRANSFERRED PAYLOAD — Noah transmitted another agent's text; this labels the transport role, not authorship of the content.
- UNADOPTED_AI_PROPOSAL — AI-generated; no evidence of Noah's adoption.
- UNKNOWN — the record does not justify a determination.

**Transmission roles** (for cross-system sequences):
- **Transmitter** — who carried the text across the boundary.
- **Conceptual originator** — who originated the idea the text expresses.
- **Drafting agent** — who produced the surface wording.
- **Adopting author** — who accepted, revised, or continued it afterward.

A human-authored concept and an AI-assisted formulation may coexist in the same artifact. Record both.
