# Citing the Continuity Intelligence Research Corpus

## Preferred corpus citation

Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Continuity Intelligence Research Corpus: Legacy.GI, Rendered Reality, ORACLE, and Governed Longitudinal Memory*. `Noahhawkes/oracle-ai-runtime`, versioned Git repository.

Because this is a living research corpus, include the Git commit SHA and access date whenever possible.

### Example

> Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Continuity Intelligence Research Corpus: Legacy.GI, Rendered Reality, ORACLE, and Governed Longitudinal Memory*. Git commit `<COMMIT_SHA>`, accessed `<YYYY-MM-DD>`.

## Artifact-level citation

Prefer artifact-level citations for technical claims.

Examples:

- Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Legacy.GI Mathematical Core v1.0*. `research/LEGACY_GI_MATHEMATICAL_CORE_V1.md`.
- Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Legacy.GI Continuity Annotation and Evaluation Protocol v0.2*. `research/LEGACY_GI_CONTINUITY_ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL_V0_2.md`.
- Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Continuity Intelligence Adversarial Evaluation: CIT-AE V1*. `research/CIT-AE-V1.md`.
- Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *The Architecture of Continuity Intelligence*. Public text-preservation edition in `research/corpus/manuscripts/`.
- Hawkes, N. A. (2026). *Cognitive World Projection and Rendered Reality: Toward a Unified Architecture for Continuity Intelligence in Human-AI Interaction*. Public text-preservation edition in `research/corpus/manuscripts/`.

## Provenance note

Some artifacts include AI-assisted drafting, mathematical formalization, critique, or methodological repair. The corpus preserves this distinction where it materially affects scientific or historical provenance. Citation to Noah A. Hawkes identifies the originating researcher and corpus curator; it should not be used to erase documented AI assistance inside a specific artifact.

## Peer-review status

Unless a separate publication record states otherwise, repository artifacts should be cited as manuscripts, technical reports, protocols, or research records rather than peer-reviewed journal publications.
