## Core falsification

- What evidence would cause abandonment or substantial narrowing of the current Light Compression Hypothesis?
- What result would show that a strong generic baseline preserves continuity as well as or better than continuity-aware compression at equal information budgets?

- What result would show that the Identity Spine architecture adds no value beyond a strong prompt-only baseline?

## Measurement

- Can independent annotators score decision, correction, temporal, relational, provenance, and uncertainty fidelity with sufficient reliability?
- Which dimensions remain stable under hostile edge cases?
- Which dimensions collapse into each other empirically and therefore should not remain separate?
- Can catastrophic continuity failures be detected reliably without hindsight?
- How should a valid UNKNOWN be scored when a model gives a plausible but unsupported answer?
- What minimum effect is practically meaningful enough to justify additional architecture?

## Human continuity

- What distinguishes legitimate personal change from reconstruction drift?
- Can another human independently recognize the same continuity structure that the represented person recognizes?
- How should relational meaning be represented without reducing relationships to metadata?
- How should uncertainty, contradiction, grief, value change, and abandoned beliefs remain visible without becoming noise?
